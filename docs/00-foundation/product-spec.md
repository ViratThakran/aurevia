# Product Specification

## Goal
Build an AI sales representative/workforce that performs repetitive sales conversations while remaining transparent that it is AI when directly asked.

## Core capabilities
- Lead outreach
- Natural voice conversation
- Discovery and qualification
- Approved product/service explanation
- Objection handling
- Appointment and follow-up scheduling
- CRM/lead updates
- Persistent lead memory
- Human handoff
- Call summaries and analytics

## Principles
1. Goal-based conversations, not rigid scripts.
2. Never claim to be human.
3. Product facts come from approved knowledge.
4. Important actions use validated tools.
5. Tenant data remains isolated.
6. Voice quality is a first-class requirement.

## Initial scope
Browser-based voice prototype first. Production telephony, compliance, billing and broad integrations come later.
